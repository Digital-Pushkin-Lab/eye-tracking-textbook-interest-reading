# Supplementary Materials: An Eye-Tracking Study of Textbook Reading

This repository contains supplementary materials for the research article:

**An eye-tracking study of textbook reading: The impact of popularisation techniques on interest and processing**

## About the Study

The study examined how interest-enhancing techniques in educational texts affect students’ reading process, their ratings of the texts, and comprehension. Eighth-grade students read four textbook passages, two in social studies (Market, Social norms) and two in geography (Relief, Soil). Each passage was presented either in its original version or in a version modified with three popularization techniques: contextualization, dialogization, and cohesion. Eye movements were recorded with an EyeLink 1000 Plus eye tracker. After each text, participants rated it and answered comprehension questions.

## Repository Contents

| File | Description |
|---|---|
| `Supplementary_Tables.pdf` | Supplementary Tables S1–S21, including descriptive statistics, mixed-effects models of version effects, prior knowledge, post-reading ratings, and comprehension results. |

## Ethics Statement

The study was approved by the Ethics Committee of the Pushkin State Russian Language Institute on 27 February 2026. Written informed consent was obtained from parents or legal guardians for their children's participation.
